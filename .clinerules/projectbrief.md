# DUKAFITI Project Brief

## Project Overview
DUKAFITI is a comprehensive business management application designed for small to medium enterprises. The application provides features for customer management, inventory tracking, sales processing, and financial reporting with offline-first capabilities.

## Core Requirements
- Offline-first architecture with sync capabilities
- Customer management with debt tracking
- Inventory management with product templates
- Sales processing with split payments
- Financial reporting and analytics
- Mobile-responsive design with PWA support
- Tutorial system for new user onboarding

## Key Features
- Customer Management: Add, edit, delete customers with credit limits and debt tracking
- Inventory Management: Product catalog with templates, stock tracking, variants
- Sales Processing: Point-of-sale system with multiple payment methods
- Debt Management: Customer debt tracking and payment recording
- Reporting: Financial reports and analytics
- Offline Support: Full functionality offline with automatic sync when online
- Mobile App: PWA with native-like experience on mobile devices

## Target Users
- Small business owners
- Retail shop managers
- Market vendors
- Service providers needing customer/debt management

## Success Metrics
- Smooth user onboarding experience
- Reliable offline functionality
- Fast performance on low-end devices
- Intuitive user interface
- Minimal data loss during sync operations
