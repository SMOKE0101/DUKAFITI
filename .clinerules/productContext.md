# DUKAFITI Product Context

## Why This Project Exists

DUKAFITI was created to solve the critical business management challenges faced by small and medium enterprises in emerging markets. Many small business owners struggle with:

- **Manual record keeping**: Using paper books or basic spreadsheets that are prone to errors and loss
- **Debt tracking issues**: Difficulty managing customer debts and following up on payments
- **Inventory chaos**: Losing track of stock levels, overstocking, or running out of popular items
- **Financial blindness**: No clear picture of business performance, profits, or cash flow
- **Offline dependency**: Need for reliable operation in areas with intermittent internet connectivity

## Problems It Solves

### 1. Business Management Simplification
- Consolidates customer, inventory, and sales management in one intuitive interface
- Eliminates the need for multiple disconnected tools or manual systems
- Provides real-time insights into business performance

### 2. Debt Management Automation
- Automatic tracking of customer debts and payment history
- Visual indicators for high-risk customers
- Streamlined payment recording process

### 3. Inventory Control
- Real-time stock level tracking
- Low stock alerts and automatic reorder suggestions
- Product template system for consistent item management

### 4. Offline-First Reliability
- Full functionality without internet connection
- Automatic sync when connectivity is restored
- No data loss during network interruptions

### 5. Mobile Accessibility
- Progressive Web App that works on any device
- Native-like experience on smartphones and tablets
- Touch-optimized interface for on-the-go business management

## How It Should Work

### User Experience Goals
1. **Intuitive Onboarding**: New users should understand core features within minutes
2. **Seamless Workflow**: Common tasks should require minimal clicks
3. **Responsive Design**: Works equally well on desktop, tablet, and mobile
4. **Offline Capability**: Users should never worry about internet connectivity
5. **Data Security**: Customer and business data is protected and private

### Core User Journeys

#### Customer Management Journey
1. Add new customers with basic contact information
2. Set credit limits and risk ratings
3. Track purchase history and outstanding debts
4. Record payments and update debt balances
5. View customer analytics and insights

#### Inventory Management Journey
1. Add products with descriptions, prices, and categories
2. Use templates for similar products
3. Track stock levels and variants
4. Receive low stock alerts
5. Process restocking and inventory adjustments

#### Sales Processing Journey
1. Quick product search and selection
2. Split payment processing (cash, mobile money, credit)
3. Automatic inventory deduction
4. Customer debt updates
5. Receipt generation and printing
